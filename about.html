ABOUT EIGHTY THREE

Bridging Strategic Vision
with Operational Mastery.

Eighty Three Ventures Private Limited is an operator-led investment and venture-building platform — capitalizing vision and engineering scale across India’s supply chain economy.

Explore Businesses > Why Choose Us

Eighty Three Ventures is built for founders, operators, and enterprises who want more than a cheque. We architect, strengthen, and scale high-impact businesses across India’s rapidly modernizing supply chain, cold-chain infrastructure, and consumer commerce ecosystem.

Founded by industry leaders with decades of direct operational leadership across global supply chain networks, logistics conglomerates, and fast-growing enterprises, we believe sustainable businesses cannot be built from spreadsheets alone. We step onto the operational floor alongside founders — aligning strategic capital with ground-level execution, governance, and long-term capability building.

Our leadership’s heritage spans organisations such as CHEP, DHL, DP World, Reliance, and LEAP India. That experience shapes how we partner: with institutional discipline, enterprise credibility, and a clear understanding of how networks, cost structures, and customer service actually work at scale.

Our model is anchored in cross-venture synergy. Rather than operating in silos, our businesses—from temperature-controlled logistics and intelligent contract warehousing to modern staple distribution—reinforce one another, generating compounding efficiencies, shared routes, and collective enterprise access.

We practice disciplined patient capital. We prioritize positive unit economics, durable economic moats, and institutional corporate governance over short-term vanity metrics—building resilient companies designed to lead their sectors for decades.

Whether we are co-building a venture from early growth or strengthening an existing platform, our approach remains the same: clarity of thesis, seriousness of execution, and partnerships built on trust. From our headquarters in Thane (W), Maharashtra, we work across India’s evolving logistics and commerce landscape with a long-term view.

WHAT DRIVES US

Principles that shape every partnership.

01

Operator-led capital

We invest and build with operators’ instincts — close to networks, customers, and unit economics — not distant from the decisions that determine whether a business scales sustainably.

02

Ecosystem over isolation

Cold chain, warehousing, logistics, and commerce create more value when connected. We design for shared infrastructure, enterprise relationships, and compounding advantage across ventures.

03

Long-term institutional building

We favour durable governance, resilient operations, and patient growth over short-lived spikes — because category leadership is earned over years, not quarters.

04

India supply-chain depth

We concentrate where we have genuine expertise: temperature-controlled logistics, distribution networks, warehousing, and consumer staples — domains where operational mastery creates lasting differentiation.

WHERE WE BUILD

Sectors we strengthen and scale.

  • Warehousing & supply-chain platforms Intelligent contract warehousing, network design, and execution-led logistics partnerships.
