LIFE AT EIGHTY THREE

Work close to real businesses.

At Eighty Three, you don’t sit far from the action. Your work touches ventures that move goods, serve markets, and compound across a connected supply-chain ecosystem.

We are building and backing companies across cold chain, logistics, warehousing, and consumer commerce. That means exposure to operations, growth, and enterprise reality — not only decks and meetings.

You will work alongside leaders with decades of experience across organisations such as CHEP, DHL, DP World, and Reliance, and across ventures like ReeferON, PAR 360, and TEN Food. Curiosity, ownership, and disciplined execution matter more than job titles alone.

WHY JOIN US

What you get when you build with us.

01

Real ownership

Take responsibility for outcomes that matter — from network coordination and process discipline to growth initiatives across portfolio companies. You will see the impact of your work in how businesses actually run, not only how they are presented.

02

Learn from operators

Work beside leaders who have built and run large-scale supply-chain and logistics businesses. Mentorship here is practical: how to think about unit economics, service levels, capacity, customers, and growth with the same seriousness.

03

India-scale impact

Contribute to infrastructure that powers cold chain, supply chain, and consumer commerce across a rapidly modernizing economy. Your work helps make food, FMCG, and temperature-sensitive goods move more reliably across markets.

04

Cross-venture exposure

Gain breadth across logistics, warehousing, food commerce, and platform businesses. Shared learning across the Eighty Three ecosystem means you grow faster than in a single siloed role — with a clearer view of how connected businesses create value.

WHO THRIVES HERE

We hire for capability and character.

  • Builders who own outcomes People who follow through, raise the standard, and stay close to execution.
  • Learners with operational curiosity Comfortable asking how things really work — costs, networks, customers, and trade-offs.
  • Collaborators across ventures Ready to work across teams and businesses without needing everything handed over.

HOW TO APPLY

A simple process.

  1. 01 Share your profile

    Email your CV and a short note on the role (or general interest) to careers@83ventures.in. Tell us what you want to build and why Eighty Three.

  2. 02 Conversation

    If there’s a potential fit, we’ll schedule a conversation to understand your experience, how you think about problems, and where you want to grow.

  3. 03 Alignment & next steps

    We’ll discuss role scope, expectations, and fit with the team. When aligned, we move forward with clear next steps — thoughtfully and respectfully of your time.
