COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need before you reach out.

What is Eighty Three Ventures (83VENTURES)?

Eighty Three Ventures is an operator-led investment firm and venture building platform based in Thane, India. We partner with founders and businesses to fund, build, strengthen and scale high-potential ventures across supply chain, cold chain, logistics, warehousing and consumer commerce.

Who can partner with 83VENTURES?

We work with ambitious founders, early-to-growth stage businesses, and entrepreneurs building in sectors where operational excellence creates lasting advantage — especially supply chain, cold chain, logistics, warehousing and consumer staples.

How is 83VENTURES different from traditional investors?

We are operator-led. Beyond capital, we bring hands-on operational expertise, network access, and execution support to help ventures build stronger systems, scale faster, and compound across a connected business ecosystem.

Which sectors does 83VENTURES focus on?

Our core focus includes supply chain, cold chain logistics, warehousing, integrated logistics platforms, and consumer commerce businesses that benefit from operational mastery and long-term building.

How can founders connect with 83VENTURES?

Email connect@83ventures.in with a short overview of the business, stage, and partnership ask — or use the contact section on our website. Our team reviews inquiries and responds when there is potential alignment.

Where is 83VENTURES headquartered?

Chirag Infotech, Office No. 605, Wagle Industrial Estate, Thane (W), Maharashtra 400604, India.

Does 83VENTURES hire talent?

Yes. We hire people who want ownership close to real operating businesses. See open roles on our Careers page, or send your CV to connect@83ventures.in.

What businesses are part of the 83VENTURES ecosystem?

Our ecosystem includes ventures such as ReeferON Cold Chain, PAR 360 Supply Chain, and TEN Food, alongside strategic investments across India’s supply-chain economy.
