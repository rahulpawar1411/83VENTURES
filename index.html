ABOUT EIGHTY THREE

Bridging Strategic Vision with Operational Mastery.

Eighty Three Ventures is an operator-led investment and venture-building platform. We architect, strengthen, and scale high-impact businesses across India’s rapidly modernizing supply chain, cold-chain infrastructure, and consumer commerce ecosystem.

Founded by industry leaders with decades of direct operational leadership, we believe sustainable businesses cannot be built from spreadsheets alone. We step onto the operational floor alongside founders — aligning strategic capital with ground-level execution.

Our model is anchored in cross-venture synergy and disciplined patient capital — building resilient companies designed to lead their sectors for decades, not chase short-term vanity metrics.
